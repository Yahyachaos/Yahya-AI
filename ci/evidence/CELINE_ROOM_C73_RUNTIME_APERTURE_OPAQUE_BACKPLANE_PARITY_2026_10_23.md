## Exact additional finding
Reading the **actual shipped optimized GLB JSON and binary accessors**, there are **two meshes and two materials**, not only the 499,998 source-derived triangles:

1. `mesh_node.010`: `499998` source-derived triangles, material `material.010`.
2. `CELINE_WindowNightDepth_BackgroundMesh`: **2 triangles** (indices `[0,1,2]`, `[0,2,3]`) forming a local quad at **z = -0.006 m**, x approximately **[-0.66,+0.66]**, y approximately **[-0.52,+0.52]**, under the *same window anchor*. Its material `CELINE_WindowNightDepth_Background` has **implicit `alphaMode=OPAQUE`**, base color `[0.012,0.018,0.024,1]`, roughness `0.32`, metallic `0.0`. Its own extras explicitly describe it as a **derived night-window depth backing**, not canonical source glass.
3. Evaluating the actual two backdrop triangles with a point-in-triangle test at the fourteen **source-empty** C62 ray sample pairs (`x=-0.075` or `+0.070`, `y=-0.50,-0.35,-0.20,0,+0.20,+0.35,+0.50`) yields **14/14 backdrop intersections**. The earlier “no triangle” result applies to the *source mesh alone* and must not be paraphrased as 14 visually transparent holes in the shipped composite.
4. Independently resampling the C63 original `empty_y_intervals` at 181 recorded x positions and 221 y positions (-0.55..0.55, 0.005 spacing) yields **6,800** source-empty grid points. The *actual two opaque backdrop triangles* cover **6,353 / 6,800 (93.43%)** of those samples using a signed-area triangle inclusion test. This is finite sampled XY overlap, **not** a continuous surface proof, alpha readback, wall-occlusion depth test, or evidence that the panel is in front of every curtain fragment.

**Interpretation:** The canonical source has physically open mesh apertures, yet the shipped window **composite** places a distinct opaque dark backing across most aperture sample rays. Moreover, C72 established an independent opaque `room_shell_back` behind/at the window region. A source-only “open aperture” test therefore cannot decide what the actual Android renderer displays, nor prove any authentic glass material. The source-PBR Blender #104 render is also **not a production-composite visual acceptance proxy**. Whether the opaque backing is intentionally retained, geometrically recut/relocated, or replaced by authorized genuine material/geometry requires **explicit Room specification/ownership judgment**, followed by real in-app proof.
